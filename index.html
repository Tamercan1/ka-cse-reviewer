Philippine Civil Service Exam Guide

Everything You Need to Know to Pass the CSE

Key information about the Career Service Examination (Professional and Subprofessional levels).

80%

Passing Rate Requirement

Examinees must attain an overall rating of at least 80.00% across all subjects to earn Civil Service eligibility.

170

Exam Items & Duration

Professional level has 170 items (3 hrs 10 mins). Subprofessional level has 165 items (2 hrs 40 mins).

5

Core Exam Subjects

Numerical, Verbal, Analytical, General Information (Phil. Constitution & RA 6713), and Clerical Operations.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is Ka-CSE Reviewer completely free to use? ▾

Yes! Ka-CSE Reviewer is 100% free with no registration, no subscription fees, and no paywalls. All practice questions, mock exams, and vocabulary digests are accessible immediately in your browser.

How does the 170-item Mock Exam work? ▾

Our full exam simulation mimics the official Civil Service Professional Examination. It presents 170 randomized questions across Numerical, Verbal, Analytical, and General Information with an active 3-hour 10-minute timer and instant passing score calculation.

What topics are covered under General Information? ▾

General Information questions cover the 1987 Philippine Constitution, Republic Act No. 6713 (Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees), Peace and Human Rights concepts, and Environmental Protection issues.

Does Ka-CSE Reviewer save my practice history? ▾

Yes! Your recent quiz scores, mock exam attempts, and completion statistics are stored privately in your browser's local storage so you can track your study improvement over time.
