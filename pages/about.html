About the Creator

Hi, I'm Tamercan.

I'm a Computer Science student who built Ka-CSE Reviewer as a simple and accessible way to prepare for the Philippine Civil Service Examination.

I wanted a reviewer that focuses on what matters: practicing questions, learning from mistakes, and preparing consistently—without unnecessary barriers like accounts or paid subscriptions.

I also personally used this project as part of my own preparation and recently passed the Civil Service Professional Examination on my first attempt.

Ka-CSE Reviewer started as a personal project, but I decided to make it available to other aspiring Civil Service professionals who might find it useful.

Good luck on your journey, future civil servant!
